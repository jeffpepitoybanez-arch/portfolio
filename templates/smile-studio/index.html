Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans and offer an in-house membership for patients without insurance. Send us your plan and we'll check your coverage before your visit.

Is teeth whitening safe for sensitive teeth?

Yes. We use a low-sensitivity gel and adjust the session length to you, so most patients feel little to no discomfort.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Most new patients are seen within the same week, and emergency slots are held open every day.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, interest-free plans are available for treatments over $500, approved in minutes.
